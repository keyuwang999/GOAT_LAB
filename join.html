    Why Join G.O.A.T LAB?

    Founding Member Opportunities

    As a newly established group, you are not just a student; you are a co-founder. You will participate deeply in building the lab culture and have a direct impact on our research directions.

    1-on-1 Mentorship

    Prof. Zhang provides hands-on guidance for every member. You will receive rigorous training in TEM techniques, academic writing, and critical scientific thinking.

    High-Impact Target

    We aim high, targeting top-tier journals. We have sufficient funding and access to world-class facilities to support your ambitious and innovative ideas.

    Open Positions

    G.O.A.T LAB is always looking for highly motivated individuals interested in Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM), In-situ Characterization, Nano-manufacturing, and High-Entropy Alloys.

    Postdoc / Research Assistant

    • Ph.D. in Physics, Materials Science, EE, or related fields.
    • Experience in TEM (especially in-situ liquid/heating) or nano-device fabrication is highly preferred.
    • Proven track record of high-quality publications.
    • Competitive salary with full support for applying to national and provincial postdoctoral funds.
